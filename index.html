Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lead time and Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
Our standard lead time is 7 working days from the confirmation of your order and completion of site measurement. The Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) is 1 unit.
How does the SGD $100 booking deposit work?
The SGD $100 deposit reserves your installation slot and covers professional on-site laser measurement. The deposit is fully offset against the final installation price quoted after measurement.
What are the warranty terms for different wire types?
We offer 3-Year Warranty for 12+1 Nylon Coated Wire ($8/sqft), 5-Year Warranty for PTFE Teflon Coated Wire ($10/sqft), and 10-Year Warranty for Nano-Coated All-Weather Wire ($16/sqft).
Can the 12+1 Nylon Wire be connected to a security alarm?
Yes! The 12+1 composite wire features 12 stainless-steel strands plus 1 internal steel conductor wire. This internal wire can be connected to an external home security alarm system for immediate intrusion alert if tampered with.
